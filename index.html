About CoDaS & Evaluation Guidelines

What is CoDaS?

CoDaS (AI Co-Data-Scientist) is a multi-agent system designed to perform end-to-end biomarker discovery from wearable sensor data. The system consists of specialized agents working together:

  • Orchestrator Agent: Coordinates the overall research workflow and manages inter-agent communication
  • Data Science Agent: Handles data preprocessing, statistical analysis, and ML modeling
  • Deep Research Agent: Conducts literature review, hypothesis formulation, and clinical interpretation

The goal of this research is to evaluate whether CoDaS can generate research outputs comparable to human experts or existing AI tools.

Evaluation Guidelines
  • You will be presented with 3 PDF outputs (Model A, B, C) - results are blinded.
  • For each model, you will rate multiple quality dimensions on a 5-point scale.
  • You will identify any errors, issues, or concerns for each output.
  • Finally, you will provide overall rankings and time estimations.
Estimated time: ~20 minutes. Your expert evaluation is invaluable for this research.
